## Distillery programme: "Where the whisky lives"

1. **Holyrood Distillery** (Edinburgh, City Centre): Edinburgh's city distillery, focused
   on experimental and innovative techniques. The home of the new wave of Scottish urban
   whisky-making.
2. **Glenkinchie Distillery** (East Lothian, near Edinburgh): The "Lowland Home of Johnnie
   Walker." A stunning Victorian-era garden distillery — classical method, classical beauty.
3. **Clydeside or Auchentoshan** (Glasgow, Clydeside): On Glasgow day: The Clydeside
   Distillery on the River Clyde, or Auchentoshan — celebrated for its rare
   triple-distillation process.

## Top 4 authentic bars: "The spirit of Edinburgh"

1. **Sheep Heid Inn**: Scotland's oldest pub, est. 1360. History soaked into every stone —
   a mandatory pilgrimage for any whisky traveller.
2. **The Black Cat**: One of Rose Street's most beloved bars — exceptional whisky
   selection paired with live folk music.
3. **Sandy Bell's**: A legendary haunt for traditional Scottish folk music and an
   outstanding range of drams. Unpretentious and utterly authentic.
4. **The Bow Bar**: Over 300 whiskies. Classic Edinburgh atmosphere. The connoisseur's
   favourite on Victoria Street.

## What's included: "The full package"

  arrival and departure.
- **Accommodation:** Hotel in Edinburgh for the full duration, including breakfast each
  morning. Centrally located in the Old Town.
- **Transfers:** All ground transport within Scotland: coach Edinburgh → Glasgow →
  Edinburgh and local transfers to distilleries.
- **Distillery visits:** Pre-booked group tours and private tastings at all three
  distilleries, with priority September reservations.
- **Private dining:** An exclusive private dinner at Whisky Collection — a curated food
  and whisky pairing in an intimate setting.
- **David as your host:** David accompanies the group throughout — guiding tastings,
  sharing stories, and opening doors standard tours don't.
